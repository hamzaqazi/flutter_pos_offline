Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CodyNest Solutions handles information when you use the Codynest POS application. It is based on the current application implementation. Codynest POS is an offline-first point-of-sale application; many shop records remain on the device unless you choose to export or back them up elsewhere.

In brief: CodyNest Solutions does not collect general shop records on its own servers. The app stores those records locally, but it does contact Firebase/Firestore for license and trial-device operations, and can optionally use Google Sign-In and Google Drive for backups. It does not contain a general Firebase Authentication, analytics, crash-reporting, or notification service integration.

1. Information handled by the app

Shop and business data

The app can store locally entered business records including products, SKUs and barcodes, prices, costs, discounts, stock, categories, sales and invoice details, returns, expenses, customers and their contact details, staff and cashier details, shop and receipt settings, held carts, and related app settings. This data is stored in local Hive storage on the device.

License information

When a license key is entered, the app sends the key to Firebase Firestore to validate it. The app receives and may cache the associated shop name, plan, activation and expiry information, and device registration state locally. The Firestore license record can include plan, active status, expiry, device registrations, maximum devices, shop name, activation details, and customer phone information as configured by the license service.

Trial information

The first-install trial lasts 14 days and requires a phone number in the trial flow. The phone number is stored locally and, when supplied, sent to Firestore with a trial-device record for WhatsApp follow-up reminders. The app also sends a device identifier, device model, trial timestamps, and trial duration to Firestore to check and prevent repeated trials on the same device.

Device information

For license and trial controls, the app obtains a platform device identifier such as an Android identifier, an iOS vendor identifier, a macOS system identifier, a Windows computer name, a Linux machine identifier, or a locally stored browser identifier where applicable. It may also send a device model or platform description with trial registration. The app does not use this information for advertising.

2. Where information is stored

Local and offline storage

Core shop records are stored in local Hive databases and can remain on the device while the app is offline. The app can continue its core sales and inventory workflow without continuous internet access. Local app storage can include the records listed above, license/trial state, backup settings, Google Drive account display details, and an optional app PIN.

Firebase and Firestore

The app initializes Firebase and uses Firestore for license documents and trial-device records. The repository does not show general cloud synchronization of products, sales, customers, staff, or other shop records to Firestore. License verification may occur when the app starts; if a verification cannot be reached, the app can rely on saved activation state under the implemented offline rules.

Google Drive

Google Drive backup is optional. If you sign in and grant the requested Drive scope, the app creates JSON backup files in a folder named "Codynest POS Backups" in your own Google Drive. It requests the drive.file scope, which is limited to files created or opened by the app rather than your whole Drive. The app can read, restore, and manage those backup files, and keeps up to two Drive backups under its current implementation. Google processes the account and Drive activity under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

3. Backups, exports, and restore

You control whether to create backups. A full JSON backup can contain products, sales, expenses, returns, customers, staff, settings, receipt settings, categories, invoice numbering, active cashier information, and relevant trial fields. Products, sales, and expenses can also be exported as CSV files for sharing through the device’s sharing features.

Optional scheduled backups are written to app-specific local storage daily or weekly, with older local backups pruned according to the setting. If Google Drive backup is enabled and a Google account is connected, an automatic backup may also be uploaded to that account. A restore imports a JSON backup and replaces current business data; the app deliberately preserves license, trial protection, backup configuration, and Drive connection settings where implemented. Keep an independent copy of important backups.

4. Device permissions

The app requests permissions only for features that need them. The exact prompt depends on platform and use:

  • Camera: used to scan product barcodes and QR codes, and for product photos where used. Camera hardware is optional on Android.
  • Bluetooth: used to discover, connect to, and print receipts on paired Bluetooth thermal printers. Android declares Bluetooth scan/connect permissions; iOS declares Bluetooth usage descriptions.
  • Location on iOS: the iOS configuration includes a When In Use location usage description for discovering nearby Bluetooth printers. Android declares Bluetooth scan with neverForLocation and does not declare location permission.
  • Internet: used for Firebase license/trial requests and optional Google Sign-In/Drive operations.
  • Files and storage: the app uses file-picking, app-specific storage, temporary files, sharing, and printing for imports, exports, and backups. Android’s current backup directory is app-specific and does not declare broad storage permission.

No notification permission is declared in the Android manifest or iOS property list reviewed for this policy.

5. Third-party services

  • Firebase Core and Cloud Firestore: initialize the Firebase project and process license and trial-device records described above.
  • Google Sign-In: authenticates the Google account you choose for optional Drive backup. Account email, display name, and profile photo URL may be cached locally for the Drive settings screen.
  • Google Drive API: stores and retrieves the app’s JSON backup files in your Drive after you grant the limited app-created-file scope.
  • Device information provider: reads the platform identifier and model data used for license and trial-device controls.
  • Bluetooth, camera, file, PDF, printing, sharing, and background-task libraries: provide local device features. The repository does not show these libraries sending business records to their vendors.

6. Sharing and user controls

You can choose whether to enter a trial phone number, activate a license, connect a Google account, enable Drive backup, create local backups, export files, or restore a backup. You can disconnect Google Drive from the app and delete local or Drive backups using the relevant device or Google Drive controls. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored records, but it does not by itself delete Firestore license or trial records or files already shared to Drive.

7. Retention

Local data remains until you delete it, clear the app, uninstall the app, overwrite it through restore, or otherwise manage it on your device. Local and Drive backup files remain until you delete or replace them under the app’s retention behavior and your own Drive controls. Firestore license and trial records are retained according to the operator’s administrative needs; the repository does not define a fixed deletion schedule.

8. Security

Codynest POS uses platform storage, Firebase transport, Google authentication, and the limited Drive scope provided by its integrations. No software can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your device, Google account, license key, PIN, exported files, and backup files. Anyone who can access an unprotected backup may be able to read its business contents.

9. Children’s privacy

Codynest POS is a business tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly design the app to collect information from children. If you believe a child provided information through the app, contact us so the relevant record can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will identify the current version. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use.
