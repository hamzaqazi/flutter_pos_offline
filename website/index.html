Tools that earn their place.

From the counter to the stock room, Codynest POS keeps the everyday work of a shop in one compact app.

01 / SELL

Fast checkout

Build carts, apply discounts and tax, record cash and change, hold carts, manage returns, and keep invoice history close.

02 / KNOW

Stock with context

Manage products, categories, prices, purchase costs, discounts, stock, SKUs, barcodes, customers, expenses, and staff cashiers.

03 / PRINT

Receipts your way

Customize receipt details and print to paired Bluetooth thermal printers. Product barcode and QR scanning is available on supported devices.

04 / WORK

Offline by design

Core shop records are stored locally, so sales and inventory work without a continuous internet connection.

05 / PROTECT

Backups that you control

Create full JSON backups, export CSV reports, restore from a file, or schedule local daily or weekly backups.

06 / EXTEND

Your Drive, optional

Sign in with Google to place app-created backups in your own Google Drive. The app requests access limited to files it creates.
