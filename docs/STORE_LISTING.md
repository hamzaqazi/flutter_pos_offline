## Full description (max 4000 chars — currently 2585)

```
Codynest POS is a fast, offline-first point of sale app built for small shops, grocery stores, pharmacies, cafés and mobile businesses. Ring up sales in seconds, keep your stock under control, print receipts on a Bluetooth thermal printer, and always know how your shop is doing — without needing an internet connection.

WHY SHOP OWNERS CHOOSE CODYNEST POS

• Works completely offline. Products, sales and reports live on your device, so billing never stops when the Wi-Fi does.
• No learning curve. A clean, modern layout your staff can pick up in minutes.
• Built for real counters. Big buttons, barcode scanning and fast checkout.

BILLING & CHECKOUT

• Add products by tapping them or by scanning a barcode
• Set quantity, price and discount on any item
• Tax settings with inclusive or exclusive pricing
• Cash payment with instant change calculation
• Attach a customer to a sale, or add a new one in two taps
• Automatic stock control — the app stops you overselling items you don't have
• Professional invoice preview you can print or share as a PDF

PRODUCTS & INVENTORY

• Products organised by categories and colours
• Add product photos straight from camera or gallery
• Barcode and SKU support for fast scanning
• Low-stock alerts with a threshold you choose
• Stock is deducted automatically after every sale

RECEIPTS & PRINTING

• Print on 58 mm or 80 mm Bluetooth thermal printers
• Customise the receipt: shop name, logo, address, phone, footer and paper width
• Share a polished PDF receipt with customers

REPORTS & INSIGHTS

• Dashboard with today's sales and all-time performance
• Sales history with search by invoice number, customer or product
• Filter sales by date range or custom period
• Track expenses and returns alongside your sales
• Export products, sales and expenses to CSV for Excel

BACKUP & RESTORE

• Automatic daily or weekly backups on your device
• One-tap backup to your own Google Drive account
• Full JSON backup and restore — move your shop to a new phone in minutes

TEAM & SECURITY

• Add staff and cashiers, set the active cashier for a shift
• Protect the app with a PIN lock
• Your business data stays on your device and in your own Drive account

GETTING STARTED

1. Add your shop name, currency and a few products
2. Start selling — no account and no credit card required
3. Connect a Bluetooth thermal printer whenever you're ready

Codynest POS begins with a free trial so you can test every feature with your own products before deciding.

Questions or feedback? Open Settings → About & Legal → Support and message us directly.
```
